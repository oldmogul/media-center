Requirements & Fees

Two pathways. One standard.

Local journalists & editors

  • Fully completed Media Council of Uganda registration form
  • Letter of introduction from the editor or employer
  • Copy of identity card issued by the employer
  • National Identity Card
  • Two colour passport-size photographs
  • Six (6) by-lined published works from the past 12 months
  • Proof of payment of registration fees

Statutory fees

Local-journalist accreditation fees are set by the Media Council of Uganda and are not published here. Write to emma@mediacentre.go.ug for the current schedule.

Source · mediacentre.go.ug/accreditation

Foreign journalists

  • Fully completed Media Council of Uganda Form G
  • Clearance letter / Certificate of good conduct from Interpol in country of origin (where no active Interpol office exists, a National Police certificate plus a cover letter from your embassy in Uganda)
  • Letter of introduction from your employer
  • Copy of identity card issued by employer
  • National Identity Card (where applicable)
  • Copy of passport — with visa page — valid for the term of accreditation
  • Two colour passport-size photographs
  • Visa / Work Permit (Special Pass) — apply online at visas.immigration.go.ug
  • Proof of payment of accreditation fees

Work portfolio · Form G section

Online media

Verifiable street address, phone & email; at least 60% original news content; updated minimum 3 times a week; six (6) by-lined articles in the past 12 months.

Independent production

Provide a letter from a broadcast organisation or film distributor committing to air or distribute the work.

Freelancers

Provide an assignment letter from a specific news organisation or publication, plus six (6) by-lined articles published in the past 12 months.

The procedure

From application to card.

A five-step pathway managed by the Secretariat at Communications House, vetted by the National Accreditation Committee.

Total turnaround · ≈ 7 working days

  1. 01

    Submit application

    Scan and email completed Form G with all attachments to emma@mediacentre.go.ug — or use the online form below.

  2. 02

    Document review

    The review team at Communications House verifies completeness and contacts you for any clarifications needed.

  3. 03

    Internal processing

    Administrative Secretary, Research & Documentation Officer and Secretary review the file in turn.

  4. 04

    Vetting

    The National Accreditation Committee vets the application, typically within seven (7) working days.

  5. 05

    Card issuance

    On approval, the Secretary signs Form G and your accreditation card is prepared for collection.

Important notice

The Media Council of Uganda reserves the right to refuse or revoke accreditation at any time. Publications which are communications outreach or advocacy publications of non-governmental or non-profit organisations do not qualify for media accreditation. Foreign work permits must clearly indicate that the applicant is coming to Uganda to do media work.
