Requirements & Fees

Two pathways. One standard.

Local Journalists & Editors

  • Fully completed Media Council of Uganda registration form
  • Letter of introduction from the editor or employer
  • Copy of identity card issued by the employer
  • National Identity Card
  • Two colour passport-size photographs
  • Six (6) by-lined published works from the past 12 months
  • Proof of payment of registration fees

The procedure

01
Submit application

Scan and email completed Form G with all attachments to emma@mediacentre.go.ug.

02
Document review

The review team at Communications House verifies completeness.

03
Internal processing

Administrative Secretary, Research Officer and Secretary review the file.

04
Vetting

National Accreditation Committee, typically within seven working days.

05
Card issuance

On approval, Form G is signed and your card is prepared for collection.
