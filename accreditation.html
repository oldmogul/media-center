Who may apply

Local journalists

  • Completed Media Council of Uganda registration form
  • Letter of introduction from the editor or employer
  • Employer identity card and National ID
  • Two colour passport-size photographs
  • Six by-lined published works from the past 12 months
  • Proof of payment of registration fees

The procedure

01
Submit

Email completed Form G with attachments to emma@mediacentre.go.ug.

02
Document review

Communications House verifies completeness.

03
Internal processing

Administrative Secretary and Research Officer review the file.

04
Vetting

National Accreditation Committee, typically within seven working days.

05
Card issuance

Collect your card once Form G is signed.

Bank wire only to M/S Media Council, ABSA Bank (Uganda) Ltd, account 0341419662, SWIFT BARCUGKX, Plot 16 Kampala Road. Cash, cheques and drafts are not accepted.
