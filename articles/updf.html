UPDF recruitment kicks off at Kololo

Ministry of Defence and Veteran Affairs · 10 August 2026

Hundreds of youths from Kampala Central, Makindye and Nakawa are taking part in the UPDF recruitment exercise at Kololo Ceremonial Grounds. The exercise is part of the ongoing recruitment of new personnel into the Uganda People’s Defence Forces.

Recruitment remains voluntary, competitive and subject to medical and academic standards set by the Force. Officers inspecting the ranks emphasised discipline, patriotism and service to the civilian population.
