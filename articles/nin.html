Cabinet adopts NIN as Tax Identification Number

By Carolyne Muyama · Ministry of ICT and National Guidance · Tuesday 1 September 2026

Hon. Justine Kasule Lumumba, the Minister of Information and National Guidance, announced strategic cabinet decisions from the 9th Cabinet meeting, held at State House, Entebbe, during a press briefing at the Uganda Media Centre this morning.

Cabinet will take September off to monitor service delivery across the country. The next Cabinet sitting will be held on 28 September 2026. “During his swearing-in, the President said this is kisanja no sleep and no corruption. We shall therefore carry out countrywide visits at all levels,” said Hon. Lumumba.

In a major policy shift, Cabinet approved the official adoption of the National Identification Number, issued by NIRA, as the standard Tax Identification Number. Replacing the outdated, largely manual tax system with a unified identity framework will address obsolete taxpayer records and revenue loss, and enable URA to improve data accuracy, streamline compliance and simplify citizens’ tax registration.

Cabinet also approved the National Water Policy 2026, and instructed the National Semi-Arid Resources Research Institute to distribute early-maturing, drought-resistant crop varieties for Karamoja. Uganda will host World Tourism Day in Mbale City on 26 September 2026, with President Museveni expected as Chief Guest.
