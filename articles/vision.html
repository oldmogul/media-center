From the Director

Recent address on the vision for the Uganda Media Centre

Alan Kasujja · Executive Director · Kampala · 2026

The Uganda Media Centre will no longer be a noticeboard. It will be the command centre of government communication — the place journalists come first, and the place citizens can trust last.

In an address to staff, accredited correspondents and ministry press officers, Executive Director Alan Kasujja set out a simple test for every release that leaves this building: is it clear, is it timely, and is it true in the language the citizen actually speaks?

“We are not involved in a PR exercise. We are involved in nation-building,” he said. “Uganda’s story must be told effectively — not just to Ugandans, but to the rest of the world.”

The Centre will deepen the Languages Desk, accelerate digital distribution, and treat the newsroom in Kampala as a home for the press, physically and virtually. Accreditation remains the gate. The record remains public.
