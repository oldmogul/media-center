Government launches emergency food relief for Karamoja

Office of the Prime Minister · 20 July 2026

The Government of Uganda has launched an emergency food relief programme to support communities in the Karamoja sub-region affected by the prolonged dry spell.

A total of 9.4 million kilograms of maize flour and dry beans will be distributed to 313,987 households across 480 parishes in the region. The exercise is coordinated by the Office of the Prime Minister in partnership with the National Enterprise Corporation, UPDF, Uganda Prisons Service, Uganda Police Force and local governments.

The Prime Minister, Rt. Hon. Robinah Nabbanja, flagged off the convoy following a directive from President Yoweri Museveni. Leaders, stakeholders and beneficiaries have been called upon to ensure relief food reaches the intended households.
