Uganda Local Organizing Committee public update on TotalEnergies CAF AFCON PAMOJA 2027

Ministry of Education and Sports · Wednesday 2 September 2026 · 4 min read
Ref: AFCON-UG 100/101/09

A lot has happened and continues to happen as we march towards the kick-off of the TotalEnergies AFCON PAMOJA 2027 tournament on 19 June 2027, co-hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda. We are about nine months away from kick-off.

The second physical inspection by CAF of the sports facilities — Akii-Bua Olympic Stadium (AKOS), Hoima City Stadium (HCS) and Mandela National Stadium (MNS) — and nine of twelve training facilities has just concluded.

Overall, Uganda is on course to have all three stadia ready by the end of December 2026, and ten of the twelve training facilities. FUFA Kadiba and Sir Tito Winyi S.S. training facilities are expected by the end of January 2027.

Government funding

The Government of Uganda has availed 100 percent of the funds required to develop all three stadia and the nine additional training facilities. Funds for the training facilities amount to Shs. 135.7 billion.

Progress at AKOS is close to 80 percent and ahead of schedule. Snags identified at Hoima City Stadium in February 2026 are nearly 95 percent complete. The UPDF Civil Engineering Brigade is the main contractor at Mandela National Stadium, with a completion date of 31 December 2026 in sight.

Business opportunities

As of today, 850 potential suppliers have registered, of which 650 have been pre-qualified. Register at afconpamoja.ug. CAF has also opened AFCON-2027 tenders at cafonline.com.
