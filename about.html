The story

Twenty years of one voice

H.E. the President established the Centre in September 2005 so government media relations would be managed professionally — outside ordinary public-service structures.

2005

The Centre opens

Plot 36A, Nile Avenue. A clearing house for government communication, built so journalists could walk in.

2013–25

The long desk

Press briefings, accreditation, languages and radio — the daily work of putting government on the record.

Dec 2025

Kasujja takes the chair

From Sanyu FM and Capital to thirteen years at the BBC World Service. The charge: tell Uganda’s story until the world can say it back.

What we do

Four charges. One Centre.

01

Brief the nation

Coordinate government press briefings and media engagements with Cabinet, MDAs and the diplomatic press.

02

Hold the record

Publish official releases, statements and announcements — and accredit the journalists who cover them.

03

Speak every language

Translate and disseminate government content in Uganda’s major languages. Monitor the discourse. Counter falsehood.

04

Project Uganda

Missions, diaspora and partners. Cabinet, Parliament, RDCs and local governments as one communication network.
