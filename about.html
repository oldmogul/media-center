About the Media Centre

We coordinate communication across every ministry of the Government of Uganda.

Our mission

To deliver clear, timely and truthful government communications to every citizen, in the language they speak.

Our Vision

A well-informed citizenry actively participating in national development through transparent and effective Government communication.

What we do

  • Coordinate Government press briefings and media engagements.
  • Publish official press releases, statements and announcements.
  • Accredit local and international journalists covering Uganda.
  • Translate and disseminate Government content in Uganda’s major languages.
  • Monitor public discourse and counter misinformation about Government.
