What we do

The clearing house for government

H.E. the President established the Centre in September 2005 so government media relations would be managed professionally — outside ordinary public-service structures, under what is now the Ministry of ICT and National Guidance.

01

Press briefings

Coordinate government press briefings and media engagements with Cabinet, MDAs and the diplomatic press.

02

The record

Publish official press releases, statements and announcements — the first draft citizens should trust.

03

Accreditation

Accredit local and international journalists covering Uganda, from State House to the parish.

04

Languages

Translate and disseminate government content in Uganda’s major languages — English, Kiswahili, Luganda and the regional tongues.

05

The discourse

Monitor public conversation and counter misinformation about government programmes and policy.

06

A fair hearing

Ensure government is fairly and objectively represented in the media, and that MDAs speak with one voice.

07

Uganda abroad

Integrate the international marketing of Uganda into the broader communication strategy — missions, diaspora, partners.

08

The network

Cabinet, Parliament, RDCs, local governments and foreign missions as critical elements of government communication.
