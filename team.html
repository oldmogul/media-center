§ Executive Director

Alan Kasujja

Executive Director · Uganda Media Centre

Seasoned Ugandan journalist with a career spanning Sanyu FM, Capital FM and a 13-year tenure at the BBC World Service, where he presented Newsday and hosted the Africa Daily podcast. Appointed by H.E. the President in December 2025, he leads government communication strategy, media relations and public messaging — coordinating engagement with the local and international press. Holds a Law Degree from the University of London.
