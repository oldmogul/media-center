About UGov Assistant

An official helper trained on the structure of Uganda’s government. It points you to the right ministry, explains public services, and helps you draft feedback in English, Luganda or Kiswahili.

Privacy & accuracy

  • Conversations are not linked to your identity.
  • Feedback transcripts are held for routing, then deleted after 30 days.
  • For legal, medical or financial matters, consult the relevant ministry or a qualified professional.
